LED Notify is a lightweight, privacy-first, zero-battery-drain notification LED indicator designed for Android devices with AMOLED / OLED displays (especially those with front camera punch-hole cutouts).

Features:
• 5 Customizable Shapes (Dot, Ring, Pill, Empty Pill & Custom ASCII / Text Symbols)
• Unified 2-finger multi-touch gestures for size & shape adjustments
• Offscreen Indicator Arrow showing exact direction if AODiode is moved offscreen
• Smart Incoming Call Dismissal for instant unobstructed call answering
• True 1Hz ECO Mode (LTPO Hardware Locking)
• Per-App Custom Colors & Whitelist/Blacklist Filter
• Advanced Settings: Timeout, Quiet Hours / DND, Low Battery Auto-Disable & Charging Indicator
• 100% Offline & Private (Zero Internet permission)
